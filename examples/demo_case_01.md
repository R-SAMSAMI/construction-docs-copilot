# Demo Case 01

## Input Context

- Project type: Bridge rehabilitation
- Work activity: Scaffold inspection and concrete patch repair
- Notes: Crew working adjacent to traffic. Access routes are tight and temporary materials are staged near the work area.

## Example Talking Points

- The app combines image review with limited field context instead of relying on image-only classification.
- The output is structured enough to support reports, supervisor review, or future workflow automation.
- The tool is positioned as decision support, not as a replacement for a qualified safety professional.

## Example Expected Themes

- PPE verification
- access and housekeeping controls
- traffic and work-zone awareness
- pre-task planning and crew coordination
